About

Born in Germany and raised in Switzerland and Japan, the artist now lives and works in Europe. They studied at the University of Applied Arts Vienna and have worked as a light sculpture artist since 1997. Alongside their artistic practice, they work as an interior and lighting concept consultant.

Their work has been recognized with the German Design Association's Best of the Best Award in the interior category (2019) and a Top 5 placement at the AHEAD Awards for Hospitality Experience and Design Europe.

Projects have been realized in Paris, Tokyo, Vienna, Rome, Osaka, Nagoya, Bucharest, Salzburg, Munich, Berlin, Milan, Zurich, and Kamakura.

Practice

Lighting designer and artist Megumi Ito approaches lamp making as more than a functional craft. For her, it is an emotional dialogue between space, traditional memory, and the interplay of light and shadow.

Her process begins with the space itself. Before creating a lamp or light installation, Ito visits the environment where it will live and studies the atmosphere of the room to sense what is missing. She then shapes the light object to fill that void and to reflect the personality of the client.

Her tactile approach to materials is rooted in her childhood in Kamakura, Japan, and she often speaks of them through an emotional lens. Educated in textile and fashion design in Vienna, she brings European design and traditional Japanese arts into conversation. Her lamps often incorporate Japanese knotting, as in her Shibari-inspired concepts, combined with contemporary iron bars and cotton ropes dyed with red cabbage.

Ito also challenges the idea that light exists purely for utility. As she puts it, “I don't believe light exists only to make things visible. Sometimes, it exists to live with the darkness.”
